**Which price would you use for competitor comparison and why?**

**I would use the total landed price: the product price plus shipping, delivered to the same location.**
The headline price isn’t what a customer actually pays. Comparing $99 (shipping included) against $89 (shipping extra) is not like-for-like. Website C only looks cheapest because its shipping cost is hidden.

**What I can conclude from the given data?**
1. Website A’s true price is $99, confirmed.
2. Website C is cheaper than A only if its shipping is under $10. At exactly $10 it ties with A, and above $10 it’s more expensive. Furniture and bulky items often cost more than that to ship, so $89 may be misleading.
3. Website B is never the cheapest. Even with free shipping, it’s $109, more than A’s $99, and shipping can only add to that.

**I won’t assume a shipping figure for C. I’d look it up for the same delivery address.**

**How I would make the comparison fair?**

1. **Compare the identical product**. Same brand, model, size, colour and specification. Check SKUs or model numbers, not just the product title.

2. **Include shipping to the same location**. Enter the same Australian postcode on every site and record the actual delivery fee at checkout. Shipping can differ by state or by regional vs. metro area.

3. **Use the same condition.**. New against new, with the same warranty. Exclude refurbished, used or imported grey-market items.

4. **Include all costs the customer pays**. Product price + shipping + any handling or payment surcharges, with GST treated consistently (all inclusive).

5. **Account for discounts consistently**. Record the regular price and the sale price separately. Note whether the “deal” needs a coupon, a membership or a minimum spend, and don’t count a price a normal customer can’t access.

6. **Check on the same date and time**. Prices change daily, so I’d collect all of them in one session and record the date.

7. **Note non-price differences**. Delivery time, returns policy, stock availability and seller type (the retailer itself vs. a third-party seller). A cheaper price with a three-week delivery isn’t directly comparable.

8. **Record the source**. Save the URL (and a screenshot where possible) for each price, so the comparison can be verified.
