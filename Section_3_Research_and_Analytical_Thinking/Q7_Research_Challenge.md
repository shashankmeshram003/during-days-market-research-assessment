1. **Amazon Australia (amazon.com.au)**

•	Website name : amazon.com.au

•	What information you can obtain : 
1. Best Sellers, New Releases, Movers & Shakers and Most Wished For lists by category.

2. Prices, review counts, star ratings and customer questions for any product.

3. Seller types (Amazon-fulfilled vs. third-party) and delivery promises.

•	Why it could be useful to During Days : 
1. Best Sellers lists show what Australians are buying at current times and what are the buyers demographics and geolocation across the country. This is super useful to during days to target high frequency geolocations and certain demographics which as high buying ratio.

2. Review counts and written reviews reveal what customers love or complain about, so you can spot product gaps.

3. Marketplaces topped online spend for the third year running. That makes Amazon a competitor to monitor.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. **eBay Australia (ebay.com.au)**

•	Website name : ebay.com.au

•	What information you can obtain :
1. Live listing prices, and, using the “Sold items” filter, to check the prices products actually sold 
for.

2. Listing volumes, seller counts and condition (new vs. used)

3. Trending searches and category pages.

•	Why it could be useful to During Days : 

1. Sold listings show real transaction prices, not just asking prices, which makes your price comparison more accurate.

2. A product with many recent sales shows genuine demand.

3. Many sellers on one product signals heavy competition.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. **Australia Post eCommerce Report (auspost.com.au/business/grow-your-business/ecommerce-reports)**

•	Website name : auspost.com.au/business/grow-your-business/ecommerce-reports

•	What information you can obtain :
1. Annual and quarterly online shopping data: total spend, households shopping, basket size, category growth, and regional and generational behaviour.

2. A record $82.6 billion spent online in 2025, with 9.8 million households shopping online, and average basket sizes shrinking to $96

3. The data comes from real parcel volumes, not just surveys. The report draws on delivery data from Australia Post Group.

•	Why it could be useful to During Days : 

1. It sizes the market and shows which categories are growing, helping you choose where to launch a new category.

2. Shrinking baskets suggest customers buy smaller items more often, which affects pricing and bundling decisions.

3. It’s free, credible and widely cited, so it’s a strong source to name in a management recommendation.



4. **Gumtree Australia (gumtree.com.au)**

•	Website name : gumtree.com.au

•	What information you can obtain :
1. Asking prices for new and used items across categories like furniture, garden, tools and home goods.

2. Resale values, meaning how much a product keeps its value after purchase.

3. Location data, showing which cities or regions have more supply or demand. Seller type, private vs. business, which hints at who the competition is.

•	Why it could be useful to During Days : 

1. Many listings for a product show it’s widely owned. Listings that disappear quickly suggest strong buyer interest.

2. A product that holds its price second-hand usually has good durability and brand trust, which lowers return and complaint risk before you commit to stock..

3.Used prices show the cheapest alternative customers have. If used items sell near your planned new price, that’s a warning on pricing. Category Ideas shows frequently listed items like storage, outdoor furniture and tools and can point to categories worth researching.

5. **PriceSpy Australia (pricespy.com.au)**

•	Website name : pricespy.com.au

•	What information you can obtain :
1. Price comparisons across many Australian retailers for the same product.

2. Price history charts showing how a product’s price has moved over time.

3. Lowest current price, number of retailers stocking it, and user reviews.


•	Why it could be useful to During Days : 

1. It shows the full competitor price spread for a product in one place, which speeds up Q4-style comparisons.

2. Price history reveals whether a “sale” is real or permanent, and when prices tend to drop (e.g. around Black Friday)

3. It shows how many retailers stock an item, which helps assess competition and set a realistic selling price.
