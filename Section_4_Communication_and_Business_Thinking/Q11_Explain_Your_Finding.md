**Recommendation to Management: Product Pricing**

Our competitor research shows that most retailers sell this product for $149-$169, while During Days can buy it for only $95. This matters because it gives us a gross margin of roughly 36-44% even at competitor prices, which is a strong profit opportunity.

I recommend launching at $139, slightly below the lowest competitor, which still earns about $44 per unit (around 32% gross margin) while attracting price-sensitive customers. I would avoid pricing much lower, as deep discounting cuts profit and can signal lower quality.

Before launch, we should verify product quality, supplier reliability, stock availability and any warranty, compliance or trademark issues. We should also calculate full costs (shipping, payment fees, marketing and returns) to confirm our true net margin, and check that competitor prices are for identical products and include shipping. Finally, I suggest a small test batch first to validate demand and the price before ordering at scale.
